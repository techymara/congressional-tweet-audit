Where to get a member’s posts
  • Copy posts from the member’s profile on x.com and paste them above, one per line.
  • Pull posts with the official X API (paid per post) and upload the JSON or CSV you save. The page looks for fields named like text, date, likes and retweets.
  • Upload tweets.js from an X data archive (only for an account you control).
  • Skip scraper tools and unofficial “Twitter APIs”. X’s terms ban scraping.

How labeling works

  • Claude reads each post with the member’s district and the category definitions you picked, then gives it one category, a confidence level and a short reason. Posts go to Claude 25 at a time.
  • One category per post keeps the shares adding up to 100%. A post that mixes legislative work with outrage goes wherever its main purpose points.
  • Labels are judgments, not facts. Check the posts under Needs review and change anything you disagree with.
  • Labeling runs on your own Claude account: your claude.ai plan when the page is opened inside claude.ai, or your Anthropic API key on the website. It takes about one request per 25 posts. Your posts and labels are saved in this browser only.
